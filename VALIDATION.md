## 5. Known limits

- Both-teams-to-score runs about 1.5-2 pp above the reference across many seasons (just outside the 2 SE band); the score-effect strength that keeps BTTS down also drives goal dispersion, and the two were balanced.
- Goals are under-dispersed as in reality, but less so than the reference at low team xG (below ~1.0): the synthetic shot mix produces slightly more 2-goal games from few chances.
- Player-level distributions (e.g. how goals spread across a squad) are plausible but not calibrated.
- Locations are realistic in aggregate; individual passes are not tracking-accurate.
- Club ratings are fixed for the season (no form swings, injuries or managerial changes).
- Reference rates come from one league's recent era; home advantage in particular drifts by era.
